# MiddleEast Dashboard

**MiddleEast Dashboard** is a locally hosted **OSINT and geospatial intelligence platform** for monitoring geopolitical developments, military activity, regional conflicts, maritime security, nuclear facilities, defence infrastructure and strategic events across the Middle East.

***

#### **Key Features**

- **17 Focus Countries:** Covers the specified countries with a 100 km surrounding geographic margin.
- **15 Intelligence Categories:** Organises regional news and developments into dedicated intelligence panels.
- **Interactive Map:** Displays airports, military-designated airfields, ports, towns, strategic sites and conflict zones.
- **Military & Naval Layers:** Includes selected US installations, naval bases and submarine facilities.
- **Nuclear & Missile Sites:** Maps selected nuclear-related facilities and missile bases.
- **Defence Industry:** Includes sourced industrial reference points.
- **Maritime Security:** Highlights strategic chokepoints, oil and gas terminals and surrounding waterways.
- **News Monitoring:** Integrates GDELT, RSS/Atom feeds and Google News search feeds.
- **Social Media Connectors:** Supports official X, Facebook and Instagram routes where configured.
- **Connector Diagnostics:** Reports source availability and configuration status.

***

#### **Geographic Coverage**

The 17 focus countries are Bahrain, Cyprus, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates and Yemen.

The map includes a **100 km surrounding margin**. Neighbouring territories appear only where they fall within that boundary.
