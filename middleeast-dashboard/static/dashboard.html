Map basis: Map basis: Natural Earth country boundaries, drawn only inside the frame. The 17 named states are shaded; their neighbours appear as outlines where they fall within 100 km of those states. The dashed line is that 100 km margin. Disputed or contested areas are described, not settled: the Golan Heights (Israeli-administered; Syria claims it), Gaza and the West Bank (Palestinian territories; the Gaza Health Ministry and government media report that Israel holds more than 63% of Gaza), the Kurdistan Region of Iraq (autonomous), the islands in the Strait of Hormuz (claimed by Iran and the UAE), the British Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia on Cyprus, and the divided island of Cyprus. Most military positions are approximate; each site's tooltip gives its source and whether it is verified in this build. Events reflect reports up to 10 October 2026, and the situation changes often. The map settles no dispute.

Sources: Ransomware.live (leak-site claims - group claims, not verified facts); Telegram threat-intel channels; and dark-web RSS sources reached through Tor that you verify yourself (data/onion_sources.json).
To read onion sources you need Tor on this PC: open Tor Browser (it listens on port 9150) or install and start the Tor service (port 9050). Keep it running, then press Refresh. Nothing is needed for the clear-web and Telegram sources. This dashboard never opens .onion pages in a browser and does not search or crawl the dark web.
